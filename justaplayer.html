Sentry / Error and Performance Monitoring

We use Sentry, provided by Functional Software, Inc., to analyze crashes, monitor performance, fix problems, and improve the Mobile Application. Sentry may collect and process the following information:

  • Error details, crash reports, and stack traces
  • Device model, operating system version, and application version
  • Performance data, including application response times and technical performance measurements
  • A randomly generated installation identifier used to distinguish app installations
  • IP address and the approximate geographic region inferred from it
  • User interface interactions and Session Replay data, which may include screen content and interactions within the Mobile Application

This information is used only for crash analysis, performance monitoring, problem resolution, and application improvement. The information is transmitted to Sentry's servers and may be processed by Sentry's service providers, including in countries other than your country of residence. For more information, please review the Sentry Privacy Policy.

Sentry retains error, performance, and Replay data for 30 or 90 days, depending on the data type and the retention period provided by our Sentry plan. Data is deleted after the applicable retention period in accordance with Sentry's policies.

You may request deletion of your Sentry diagnostic data before the retention period expires by emailing gao.xue.qi1@gmail.com. Please include your application version, the approximate date and time of the relevant event, and your random installation ID if available, so that we can locate the data. We may need to verify your request before completing deletion.
