WhoCalled Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the WhoCalled Android application ("WhoCalled", "the app", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and discloses information. By using WhoCalled, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

Important: WhoCalled records notifications only after you grant notification access. Notification content may contain personal or sensitive information, such as sender names, messages, or verification codes. Notification records are stored in the app's private local database and are not uploaded to a server operated by us. However, Sentry, our diagnostics provider, may receive diagnostic data and crash screenshots that could include information visible in the app, as explained in Section 4.

1. Information WhoCalled Processes

1.1 Notification Information

When you enable notification access, WhoCalled can read and save notifications posted by other apps. The information processed may include:

  • The source app's name and package name
  • Notification titles, text, expanded text, subtext, summaries, information text, and message lines
  • The date and time each notification was posted
  • Statistics generated from these records, including daily notification counts and the apps that post the most notifications

WhoCalled records notification content as provided by Android and does not filter out personal or sensitive content. Please consider this before enabling notification access.

1.2 App and Device Information

WhoCalled uses Android's package visibility capability to identify the names of apps that post notifications. The app does not require an account and does not ask you to create a username or password. Sentry may collect additional device and diagnostic information as described in Section 4. Google AdMob may collect device and advertising information as described in Section 5.

1.3 Information You Voluntarily Provide

If you contact us for support, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address and the contents of your message.

2. How We Use Information

We use information for the following purposes:

  • To display your notification history
  • To calculate and display notification statistics
  • To provide, maintain, and improve the app's functionality
  • To diagnose errors, monitor performance, and fix crashes
  • To display and measure advertisements through Google AdMob
  • To respond to support requests
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

3. Local Storage, Android Backups, and Retention

Notification records are stored in a private SQLite database on your device. Other apps cannot directly access this database under Android's standard security model. We do not operate a server that receives a copy of your notification database.

Records remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. You can delete all locally stored notification history and statistics by selecting Clear data in the app's settings. Revoking notification access stops future notification collection but does not delete records that have already been saved.

Android's backup and restore features may back up app data, including the notification database, to your device's backup provider, depending on your Android version, device, and backup settings. That provider is typically Google. Backup copies are governed by the provider's own privacy and retention policies and may not be deleted immediately when you clear or uninstall the app. You can manage backup settings through your device or backup provider.

4. Sentry Diagnostics and Performance Monitoring

WhoCalled uses Sentry, a third-party service provided by Functional Software, Inc., to identify technical problems, monitor app performance, and improve stability. Sentry may automatically receive and process:

  • Crash reports, stack traces, error details, and diagnostic logs
  • Device model, operating system version, app version, and related technical information
  • IP address and information derived from it
  • App usage events, performance measurements, and user-interaction breadcrumbs, such as taps, swipes, and scrolling
  • Screenshots and view-hierarchy information attached to crash reports

A crash screenshot or view hierarchy may contain notification content or other personal information visible on the screen when the crash occurs. This diagnostic information is transmitted to Sentry rather than retained only on your device. Sentry processes information in accordance with its own privacy policy:

  • Sentry Privacy Policy
  • Sentry Android Data Collection Documentation

5. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

WhoCalled uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC, to display advertisements. AdMob and its partners may automatically collect and process information to provide, personalize, and measure ads, and to prevent fraud and abuse. This information may include:

  • Your Android Advertising ID and similar device or app identifiers
  • IP address and approximate, non-precise location derived from it
  • Device model, operating system version, language, and app-related technical information
  • Information about ads shown to you and your interactions with those ads
  • Advertising performance, diagnostic, and fraud-prevention data

Depending on your region, settings, and available consent choices, AdMob may show personalized ads based on your interests and activity or non-personalized ads based on contextual information. Advertising data collected by Google is retained in accordance with Google's own policies.

You can limit or manage ad personalization through Android's advertising settings and your Google account. Turning off ad personalization does not necessarily stop ads from appearing. For more information, visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Ads Settings

6. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information for money. We do not provide our locally stored notification database to AdMob or other advertising networks. We may disclose information in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Sentry processes diagnostic and performance information on our behalf as described in Section 4
  • Advertising Partners: Google AdMob processes device, advertising, and interaction data as described in Section 5
  • Backup Providers: Your Android backup provider may process app data when system backup is enabled
  • Legal Requirements: When disclosure is necessary to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or a valid government request
  • Protection of Rights and Safety: When necessary to protect our rights, users, systems, or the safety of others
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or transfer of assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections

Some advertising data processing may be considered "sharing" under certain privacy laws even though we do not sell your personal information for money. You can exercise any applicable opt-out rights using the advertising controls described in Section 5 or by contacting us.

7. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

You can control your information in the following ways:

  • Disable Notification Access: Open Android's notification access settings and turn off access for WhoCalled. This prevents future collection but does not erase existing records.
  • Delete Local Records: Use Clear data in WhoCalled's settings, clear the app's storage through Android settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Manage Backups: Change or disable backup options in your device or backup account settings.
  • Manage Ad Personalization: Use the Android and Google advertising controls described in Section 5. You can also delete or reset your Android Advertising ID through your device settings, where supported.
  • Contact Us: Request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold, including support correspondence or identifiable diagnostic data, subject to applicable law.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to object to or restrict certain processing, request a copy of your personal information, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

8. Legal Bases for Processing

If the General Data Protection Regulation or a similar law applies, our legal bases depend on the context:

  • Performance of a Contract: To provide the notification history and statistics features you choose to use
  • Legitimate Interests: To maintain security, diagnose failures, monitor performance, improve the app, and display non-personalized advertising, balanced against your rights and expectations
  • Consent: For personalized advertising where required and for other processing based on your consent, which you may withdraw at any time
  • Legal Obligations: To comply with applicable laws and lawful requests

9. International Data Transfers

Sentry, Google AdMob, and your device backup provider may process information in countries other than the one in which you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers in accordance with applicable data protection laws and the relevant provider's policies.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information, including Android's private app storage for notification records. However, no app, electronic storage system, or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should protect your device and consider the sensitivity of notification content before enabling notification access or system backups.

11. Children's Privacy

WhoCalled is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the app, our practices, or applicable law. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
