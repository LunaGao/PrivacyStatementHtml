Spectrum Puzzle Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 22, 2026

Important Notice: Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using Spectrum Puzzle, you agree to the collection, use, and protection of your information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

Spectrum Puzzle is a puzzle game that does not require account registration. We do not ask you to provide a username, email address, password, or any other personal information to play the game. The only information you may voluntarily provide is when you contact us directly for support (for example, your email address and the content of your message).

1.2 Information We and Our Partners Collect Automatically

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and similar technical information.
  • Usage Information: Game feature usage, error and crash logs, and other diagnostic data used to improve stability.
  • Advertising Information: Our advertising partner, Google AdMob, may automatically collect your device's advertising identifier (such as Apple's IDFA or the Android Advertising ID), IP address, approximate location, and information about your interactions with ads. See Section 3 for details.

1.3 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Your game progress, completed levels, scores, and settings are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the game
  • Display advertisements that keep the game free to play
  • Diagnose and fix bugs and crashes
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Spectrum Puzzle uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google LLC, to display advertisements in the game. AdMob may collect and process certain data from your device in order to serve and measure ads, including:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
  • IP address and approximate (non-precise) location
  • Device and app information
  • Information about ads shown to you and your interactions with them

3.1 Personalized and Non-Personalized Ads

Depending on your region and your choices, AdMob may show you personalized ads (based on your interests and activity) or non-personalized ads (based only on contextual information, such as the content of the game). If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we will ask for your consent before showing you personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time through the consent options in the game.

3.2 How to Limit Ad Personalization

You can limit or disable ad personalization at any time through your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising
  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of Ads Personalization

For more information about how Google collects and processes data, please see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Ads Settings, where you can manage ad personalization

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties, and we do not share your information except in the following circumstances:

  • Advertising Partners: Google AdMob processes certain device and advertising data as described in Section 3 in order to serve ads in the game
  • Legal Requirements: To comply with laws, regulations, court orders, or government requests
  • Protection of Rights: To protect our rights or the rights, property, or safety of others
  • Business Transfers: In connection with business transactions such as mergers, acquisitions, or asset sales
  • Service Providers: With trusted third parties (such as crash-reporting services) who help us operate the game and are bound to protect your information

5. Data Security

We take the security of your information seriously:

  • Game progress and settings are stored only locally on your device
  • Any diagnostic or advertising data transmitted is protected using encryption during transmission
  • We collect only the minimum data necessary to operate and improve the game

6. Data Retention

Locally stored game data remains on your device until you delete the game or clear its data. Diagnostic and support information we hold is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained in accordance with Google's own data retention policies.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request the personal information we hold about you (typically limited to support correspondence)
  • Right to Deletion: Delete all locally stored game data at any time by uninstalling the game, and request deletion of any information we hold about you
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw previously given consent at any time, including consent to personalized advertising through the options described in Section 3

8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • Right to Know: Request information about the categories and specific pieces of personal information we collect
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt-out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. We do not sell your personal information; however, advertising identifiers processed by AdMob may be considered "sharing" under the CPRA. You can opt out at any time using the device settings described in Section 3.2
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Exercise these rights without facing discrimination

To exercise these rights, contact us using the information in Section 11.

9. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. Spectrum Puzzle collects no account or contact information from any player. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us (for example, by contacting support), please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Policy Updates
